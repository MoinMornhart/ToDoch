Calendar & subscriptions

ToDoch has its own calendar with month, week, day and agenda. It works with other calendars in both directions: your events can be shown elsewhere, and other calendars – like Streamo – can be brought into ToDoch.

Your events in Apple, Google or Outlook

Under Areas → Subscribe to calendar (ICS) you create a secret link – for all areas or just one, with all details, titles only or just “busy”. One click opens it in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook; every link can be revoked on its own.

Google and Outlook.com fetch the calendar from the internet – ToDoch has to be publicly reachable for that, e.g. behind a reverse proxy.

Streamo × ToDoch

Streamo provides a calendar with everything you have planned: films (with time and running time), watch plans, new episodes and titles about to leave a subscription.

  1. In Streamo, open the Calendar tab and copy the subscription address (http://<streamo>:3000/api/public/calendar/…ics).
  2. Optionally create an area called “Streamo” in ToDoch – the form then picks it automatically.
  3. Under Areas → Add calendars, enter a name and the address, choose how often to sync (15 minutes to daily) and click Add.

From then on ToDoch syncs in the background, and instantly at the push of a button: new events are added, changed ones updated, removed ones deleted. These events are read-only in ToDoch – you change them in Streamo.

Streamo usually runs in your home network. Only an admin can add such addresses. If the copied address contains localhost, replace it with the Streamo container's IP – otherwise ToDoch can't reach it.

Adding other calendars

Any ICS address (https://, http:// or webcal://) works the same way – public holidays, school holidays, a work calendar. Recurrences and exceptions are imported; times without a time zone use the time zone of your profile.

Reminders

Enable Settings → Reminders as notifications on each device. ToDoch then notifies you before events via push, even when the app is closed – on iPhone after “Add to Home Screen”. The server generates the required keys itself.
