Your to-dos and appointments.
On your own server.

Minimalist, fast from the keyboard, secure by default. Tasks, calendar and reminders in one app that runs on your Proxmox – no cloud, no tracking.

Install on Proxmox Read the docs View on GitHub

Installed in a few minutes

Run it on the Proxmox VE host – in the style of the community scripts: it creates a Debian container, installs Docker, generates all keys and shows you the setup link at the end.

bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MoinMornhart/Todoch/main/ct/todoch.sh)"

Updating? Just type update inside the container – with a backup first and automatic rollback. Without Proxmox it works with Docker Compose. Installation guide →

What's inside

🏠

Overview

Everything at a glance when you open it: due today, overdue, next events, what's coming up and how much is open in each area.

⚡

Quick add

Type one line and ToDoch detects date, time, priority, tags, area and recurrence. In English and German: “Pay bill tomorrow 2pm !high #finance @work”.

📝

Tasks as tickets

A complete form with checklist, Markdown notes, priority, tags and recurrence – daily, on weekdays, monthly, yearly.

🗓️

Calendar

Month, week, day and agenda, drag & drop, series with “only this / following / all”, fixed appointments and overlap warnings.

🗂️

Areas

Work, Private and your own areas with colour and icon. Each area has its own calendar days and hours – e.g. work only Mon–Fri, 8–18.

🍿

Streamo × ToDoch

Planned films, watch plans and new episodes from Streamo show up in your calendar automatically – and so does any other ICS calendar.

📬

Email

Add a mailbox via IMAP, read your mail as safe plain text and turn any email into a task with one click. ToDoch only reads along – nothing changes on the server.

📡

ICS subscriptions

Secret links for Apple, Google and Outlook – for all areas or one, with all details, titles only or just “busy”.

🔔

Reminders

Web push on phone and desktop, even when the app is closed. The server generates the keys itself.

🔑

Passkeys

Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or a security key – no username, no password.

⌨️

Keyboard first

n new task, c new event, / search, j/k navigate, x done, ? all shortcuts.

🌙

Light & dark, EN & DE

Dark mode with one click or following your system, English and German – switchable on the sign-in page, server messages included.

📱

Everywhere

Installable as an app (PWA) on phone and desktop, with a ➕ button and a read-only view when you're offline.

What it looks like

🍿 Streamo × ToDoch

Streamo knows which films you plan to watch, when new episodes air and what is about to leave your subscriptions – ToDoch brings all of that into your calendar automatically.

  1. In Streamo, copy the subscription address under Calendar.
  2. In ToDoch, paste it under Areas → Add calendars and pick an area, e.g. “Streamo”.
  3. Done – ToDoch syncs in the background. Plan a film for Saturday 8:15 pm and it shows up in your calendar shortly after, with its running time.

🔒 Secure by default

Passkeys, Argon2id passwords checked against leak lists, server-side sessions, a strict Content Security Policy without unsafe-inline, CSRF and SSRF protection, encrypted credentials, an audit log that cannot be altered afterwards and non-root containers. Your data stays on your server.
